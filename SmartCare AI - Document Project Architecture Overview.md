**(Overview: Overview and Executive Summary) 1)

The **SmartCare AI** platform is an integrated smart healthcare system designed to redefine and automate all stages of the patient journey, from initial assessment to long-term follow-up and complete recovery after procedures or treatments.

For clients/patients and control panels The advanced platform for physicians relies primarily on the precise integration of mobile applications (predictive telemedicine, in addition to a telemedicine unit) for artificial intelligence and analysis, hospitals, and AI engines and management.
